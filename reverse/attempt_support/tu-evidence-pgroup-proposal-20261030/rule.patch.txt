    p-group: a funclet group follows its parent, never leads it
    
    Symptom (cmi1, reverse/re_attempts.log "tu-merge:...ImmortalBody.cpp",
    "...Behavior/UpgradeSoundSelectorClientBehavior.cpp"): the map approved
    33 .text$x funclets 0x0078FB39..0x0078FCD4 into
    Body/ImmortalBody.cpp, 31 of them with SymbioticStructuresBody,
    RespawnBody, DelayedDeathBody, FreeLifeBody, DetachableRiderBody,
    OathbreakerBody and PorcupineFormationBodyModule parents. It also
    approved 11 funclets into Behavior/UpgradeSoundSelectorClientBehavior.cpp,
    among them the getModuleNameKey $L310 rows 0x00771C54/0x00771C6B, whose
    parents 0x00253DB2/0x00253E8E are there by U while their own N names
    (Horde)NotifyTargetsOfImminentProbableCrushingUpdate.cpp. A merge
    trial pulled three $L310 rows into one source (check_csv refused it)
    and would fold seven classes into ImmortalBody.cpp.
    
    Root cause: tu_evidence.apply's P_GROUPS exception approves a funclet
    of a proposed parent with the parent's TU and skips every check on
    that TU. Both TUs are noncontiguous: StructureBody.cpp's rows
    interrupt ImmortalBody.cpp's span, and tu_map approves none of their
    rows. tu_skeleton absorbs a donor whose rows are all assigned to the
    TU at any confidence. An approved funclet therefore brings its
    proposed parent's file along, so the parent moves because of the
    funclet's approval. The hold-out that measured P_GROUPS cannot see
    this: the wrongly grouped parents have no truth label.
    
    Fix: group_refused(). A group joins no TU the map marks noncontiguous
    and follows no parent whose own evidence names another file
    (`contradicted`, or by U). U also covers ArmySummary's 10 funclets.
    They were grouped into WWLib/ArmySummary.cpp and
    Common/ArmySummaryEntry.cpp after U parents whose W lead names
    GameLogic/System/ArmySummary.cpp. A refused funclet keeps its TU as
    proposed (by P, no p-group token), the same as with P_GROUPS off.
