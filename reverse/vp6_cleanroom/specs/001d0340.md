## Behaviour

SSE2 version of 0x001C8A30 with the same observable behaviour: the deblock loop limit for the frame q index is replicated into eight lanes, for each block, the horizontal edge above it (q is the scale entry for the quality index of the block directly below the edge, upper side's activity to the block above, lower side's to the block below) and then, for every block but the first, the vertical edge on its left in place on the destination eight rows up (q is the scale entry for the quality index of the right-hand block, left side's activity to the left block's accumulator and right side's to the right block's). Smoothed lines receive the edge model's nine-tap averages, other lines receive the four-tap loop-filter fallback bounded by the loop limit with the remaining six pixels copied. Eight 16-bit lanes per step (limit: multiplies at 0x001D0418-0x001D0431 then arithmetic shift right 5 at 0x001D043B).

Edge model shared by the variance-based band deblockers (one line crossing a block edge, eight edge pixels: four on each side, plus one further pixel outward on each side, called the outer samples, q is the quantiser step of the block below/right of the edge):
- smoothing limit = (3 x q x q) shifted right 5 (retail: two 16-bit multiplies by q with the 16-bit 3s constant then a logical shift right 5, e.g. 0x001C770C-0x001C7712 and 0x001C8AF1-0x001C8B14).
- outer taps: on each side the outer sample is used if its absolute difference from the adjacent edge pixel is below q, otherwise that adjacent edge pixel is used instead.
- side activity: for each side, the sum of its four pixels and the sum of their squares are formed and the activity is the sum of squares minus the product of (sum halved, rounded down) and (sum plus one, halved, rounded down). The activity of the first side is added into the first block's accumulator at +0x28, that of the second side into the second block's.
- a line is 'smoothed' when both activities are below the smoothing limit and the absolute difference of the two pixels touching the edge is below q.
- smoothing: each of the eight edge pixels is replaced by a symmetric nine-tap weighted average centred on it with weights 1, 1, 2, 2, 4, 2, 2, 1, 1 (total 16, rounding constant 8 is applied as the 16-bit 4s constant added before a doubling, final arithmetic shift right 4 at e.g. 0x001C7AC5 / 0x001CF832), window positions that fall beyond the first or last edge pixel take the value of the corresponding outer tap. Results are stored as bytes (unsigned pack).

- a line that is not smoothed receives the four-tap loop filter instead: a correction equal to (first-side inner-but-one pixel minus three times the first-side edge pixel plus three times the second-side edge pixel minus the second-side inner-but-one pixel, plus 4) arithmetically shifted right 3, bounded with the deblock loop limit (the uint32 table whose pointer is stored at 0x00E22CEC, entry [frame q index]), the two pixels touching the edge are moved towards each other by that amount (added on the first side, subtracted on the second, clamped to 0..255) and the other six pixels of the line are copied unchanged.
Bounding rule used by the loop filters in this library (named 'bound' below): a correction value is kept unchanged while its magnitude is below the limit, is reduced linearly beyond that so that a magnitude of twice the limit maps to zero (the response is the limit minus the distance of the magnitude from the limit), and is zero for magnitudes of twice the limit or more, the sign of the original value is preserved. The SIMD code realises this with arithmetic shifts by 15 to extract signs, saturating subtractions and a multiply by a sign mask built from the replicated 16-bit ones.
The eight 1s (0x00DB80F0) and 4s (0x00DB80A0) implement the bound and the rounding of the fallback filter.
