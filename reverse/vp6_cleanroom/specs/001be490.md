Behaviour:
Vertical-edge pass of the newer deblocker for one band. The quantizer step is the scale-table entry for the frame Q index (0x001BE497..0x001BE49E) and the spread limit is the step squared, times 3, arithmetically shifted right 5 (0x001BE4BE..0x001BE4C4). The routine processes the vertical edges between horizontally adjacent fragments, that is (fragments across minus 1) edges, the first lying 8 pixels into the band (count set at 0x001BE4AA..0x001BE4E0, nothing happens when fewer than two fragments are across). No fragment-spread accumulators are touched by this routine.
For each edge and each of the 8 lines, ten consecutive samples are read: five left of the edge and five right. Two activity measures are formed: the sum of the absolute differences between neighbouring samples over the five left samples (four differences) and the same over the five right samples (0x001BE53B..0x001BE609). The line is smoothed when both activity measures are below the spread limit and the two samples adjacent to the edge differ by less than the quantizer step in both directions (0x001BE611..0x001BE63B). Where it applies, the flat smoothing replaces each of the eight run samples by the sum of the three samples to its left, the three to its right and twice itself, plus 4, arithmetically shifted right 3. Window positions outside the run on the left all take the value of the sample five positions before the edge, and those outside on the right take the sample four positions after the edge (these two outer samples are read in addition to the run). All inputs are original values (rounding at 0x001BE654, shift at 0x001BE65A and following). The eight results are written to the destination at the eight run positions.
When the line is not smoothed only the two samples adjacent to the edge are written (destination positions 7 and 8 of the fragment pair, 0x001BE71C and 0x001BE725): When the line is not smoothed, only the two samples adjacent to the edge change: a difference is formed from the four samples nearest the edge as (second-left) minus three times (first-left) plus three times (first-right) minus (second-right), 4 is added and the result arithmetically shifted right 3. That value indexes the bounding array whose centre pointer is field 0x38 and the looked-up amount is added to the first-left sample and subtracted from the first-right sample, each result taken through the clamp table (0x001BE6F9..0x001BE70D).
