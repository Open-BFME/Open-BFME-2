rva: 0x001C22C0
size: 1526
ret_site: 0x001C28B5
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] instance (unused), [esp+8] source block pointer, [esp+0xC] destination block pointer, [esp+0x10] pitch, [esp+0x14] fragment Q index, [esp+0x18] pointer to a 64-dword quantizer scale table
returns: void
callees: none
tables: 0x00DB7A28 sharpen table (64 signed dwords by Q index)
fields: none (two local 16-bit arrays of 72 entries hold the vertical and horizontal neighbour weights)

Behaviour:
Milder variant of the adaptive de-ringing of DeringBlockStrong_C. Differences:
Weights: the cap is three times the scale-table entry for the Q index, limited to 24 instead of 32, and each neighbour weight is the entry plus 32 minus twice the absolute difference between the two pixels of the pair (instead of once), with the same sharpen-below-minus-64 rule and clamping. As in the strong filter, all vertical and horizontal weights are computed from the unmodified source before any output is written.
Output blend: identical (four edge neighbours weighted, pixel weighted by 128 minus the sum, plus 64, shifted right 7, clamped).
Store order and aliasing: unlike the strong filter, this routine stores each output pixel as soon as it is computed (first store of a row at 0x001C26A9, the next pixel's neighbour loads at 0x001C26D9 and 0x001C2700 follow, next store at 0x001C2730). When the destination aliases the source, a pixel's left neighbour is therefore read after being filtered, and the row above is read after it was filtered. With disjoint buffers the two orders give identical results.
