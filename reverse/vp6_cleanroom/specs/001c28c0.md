Behaviour:
Eight-neighbour de-ringing of an 8x8 block. The strength value is the scale-table entry for the Q index, except that when the post-processing level exceeds 100 the strength is the level minus 100 (level test at 0x001C28DF..0x001C28F8). The neighbour-difference penalty is twice the absolute difference between the pixel and the neighbour when the spread measure is above 2048 and at most 32768, and the absolute difference itself otherwise (the integer factor 8 or 4 is selected at 0x001C28FE..0x001C2912, the absolute difference is multiplied by it and arithmetically shifted right 2 at 0x001C29F3..0x001C29FC). The weight cap is three times the strength, limited to 32 (0x001C291A..0x001C291D).
For every pixel of the block, each of its eight surrounding pixels (the 3x3 ring) receives a weight equal to the strength plus 32 minus the penalty for that neighbour, a weight below -64 becomes the sharpen-table entry for the Q index, otherwise it is clamped to 0 from below and to the cap from above. The output pixel is the pixel times (256 minus the sum of the eight weights) plus each neighbour times its weight, plus 128, arithmetically shifted right 8 and clamped to 0..255. The block reads one pixel of border on each side and writes only the 8x8 block.
