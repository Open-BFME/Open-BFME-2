name: DeblockFrameInterlaced
rva: 0x001BFD10
size: 368
ret_site: 0x001BFE7F
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to POSTPROC_INSTANCE (retail post-processor instance), [esp+8] source frame, [esp+0xC] destination frame
returns: void
callees: 0x001BD590 or the pointer at 0x00E22F80 (array builder by version, as in SetupDeblocker), 0x001BFAC0 DeblockPlaneNew (four times)
tables: 0x00E22CEC, 0x00E22CF0, 0x00E22CE8
fields: as DeblockFrame plus 0x84 Y plane fragments, 0x88 UV plane fragments

Behaviour:
Builds the deblock bounding array for the frame Q index (generic builder when version is at least 2, else the platform pointer) into field 0x38, zeroes the fragment variances. Deblocks the Y plane as two fields: DeblockPlaneNew with line step twice the Y stride, start 0, horizontal fragments across, vertical fragments halved down, planes at recon Y offset, Y scale. Then temporarily advances the fragment variance pointer by (horizontal times vertical fragments) halved entries and runs the same on the odd field (planes at recon Y offset plus one Y stride). Restores the variance pointer. Then U with (UV stride, start = Y plane fragments, fragment counts halved, recon U offset, UV scale) and V with start = Y plus UV plane fragments at recon V offset.
