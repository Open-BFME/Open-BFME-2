Behaviour:
In-place vertical 3-to-5 interpolation of one band: rows 0, 1, 2 hold three source rows and row 5 holds the next band's first source row. Per column, using the original values: row 1 becomes 102 times row 0 plus 154 times row 1, row 2 becomes 205 times row 1 plus 51 times row 2, row 3 becomes 51 times row 1 plus 205 times row 2, row 4 becomes 154 times row 2 plus 102 times row 5. Each weighted sum has 128 added and is shifted right 8 before being stored as a byte. Row 0 is unchanged. Nothing happens when the width is 0.
