Behaviour:
Finds the nearest vectors for frame 1, decodes the mode with the last mode and the returned type, stores it as the last mode, into the prediction-mode byte for the (row times columns plus column) and as the macroblock mode.
Mode 7 (four vectors): block modes 0..3 are each decoded with VP6_DecodeBlockMode, block modes 4 and 5 are 7. For k in 0..3: block mode 0 gives a zero vector, 3 gives the nearest inter vector, 4 the near inter vector, 2 a decoded vector (mode 2), vectors from modes 3, 4 and 2 are summed. Each summed component is rounded: (sum plus 1 plus (1 when non-negative)) arithmetically shifted right 2. The macroblock vector becomes block vector 3, and block vectors 4 and 5 become the rounded average.
Other modes: 3 gives the nearest inter vector, 4 the near inter vector, 8 finds the golden nearest vectors then uses the nearest golden vector, 9 likewise the near golden vector, 2 decodes a vector with mode 2, 6 finds the golden nearest vectors then decodes a vector with mode 6, and modes 0, 1 and 5 give zero. The result is stored as the macroblock vector and into all six block vectors, and all six block modes are set to the mode.
