## Behaviour

Executes one SSE2 instruction (a packed-double exclusive-or of a 128-bit register with itself, at 0x001D556D) inside a structured exception frame and returns 1. If the instruction faults (SSE2 not supported or not enabled by the OS), the filter at 0x001D558E selects the handler, which returns 0. No references to this routine exist in retail.
