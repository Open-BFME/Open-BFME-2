name: VP6_DeleteTmpBuffers
rva: 0x001B5530
size: 166
ret_site: 0x001B55D5 (followed by CC padding)
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to PB_INSTANCE (retail decoder instance, 0x4954 bytes)
returns: void
callees: 0x001B6410 duck_free (one pointer argument, caller cleans)
tables: none
fields: 0x274 raw allocation of the 64-coefficient reconstruction buffer, 0x270 its aligned pointer
        0x28C raw allocation of the loop-filtered block buffer (256 bytes), 0x290 its aligned pointer
        0x288 raw allocation of the temporary data buffer (64 shorts), 0x284 its aligned pointer
        0x280 raw allocation of the temporary reconstruction buffer (64 shorts), 0x27C its aligned pointer
        0x268 raw allocation of the scale buffer, 0x264 its aligned pointer

Behaviour:
Releases the five scratch buffers owned by the decoder instance and clears both the raw and the aligned pointer of each.
Order of the free calls: field 0x274, then 0x28C, then 0x288, then 0x280, then 0x268. Each free is skipped when the raw pointer is null.
After the frees, ten dword fields are zeroed in this store order: 0x274, 0x28C, 0x288, 0x280, 0x268, 0x270, 0x290, 0x284, 0x27C, 0x264.
Memory goes through the duck-memory shim: allocate = 0x001B6400 (size, tag), free = 0x001B6410. Every aligned buffer is kept twice: the raw allocation pointer and the pointer rounded up to the next 32-byte boundary (add 31, clear low five bits).
