## Behaviour

Horizontal 3-to-5 upscale of one line. For each group of three source pixels a, b, c (with d the first pixel of the next group), five outputs are produced: a, (102a + 154b + 128) shifted right 8, (205b + 51c + 128) shifted right 8, (51b + 205c + 128) shifted right 8, (154c + 102d + 128) shifted right 8. Groups are processed while the group start is below (source width - 3), for the final group the fifth output is c instead of a blend. MMX form: 4 products at a time with the two weight vectors listed, the loop processes one group per step.
