name: SetupDeblockValueArray_Generic
rva: 0x001BD590
size: 145
ret_site: 0x001BD620 (also 0x001BD61A)
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to POSTPROC_INSTANCE (retail post-processor instance), [esp+8] signed limit
returns: pointer to the centre (entry 256) of the deblock bounding array
callees: none
tables: none
fields: 0x3C deblock bounding array (512 signed dwords)

Behaviour:
Builds the bounding function used by the deblockers as a table of 512 signed dwords addressed from its centre. All entries are first cleared (0x001BD5A7). Then, for a positive limit L, the entries within L of the centre reproduce their own signed offset (an offset d between minus L and L maps to d), and the entries between L and 2 L on either side fall back linearly toward zero (an offset d with L below d below 2 L maps to 2 L minus d, and symmetrically for negative offsets), so the function is a tent that peaks at plus or minus L and vanishes at plus or minus 2 L and beyond (stores at 0x001BD5E4, 0x001BD5E6, 0x001BD5E9, 0x001BD5F3, the entries at exactly plus or minus 2 L stay zero). A limit of zero or less leaves the array all zero. Returns the centre pointer.
