Behaviour:
Band deblocker of the newer profile. The quantizer step is the scale-table entry for the frame Q index (0x001BF0A5..0x001BF0B0) and the activity limit is three times the step, arithmetically shifted right 2 (0x001BF157). Nothing happens when fragments across is 0.
The band pointer addresses the first row below a horizontal fragment boundary, so the fragment whose rows begin there lies physically below the boundary and is indexed by the starting index plus the fragment's offset plus fragments across, while the starting index plus offset names the fragment above the boundary (previous band). For each fragment offset in order (0 to fragments across minus 1):
Horizontal edge at the top of the band. For each of the 8 columns, ten vertical samples are read: five rows above the band boundary and five from it downward. The upper and lower activity measures are the sums of absolute differences between vertically adjacent samples within the five above and within the five below. Each measure, capped at 255 (0x001BF345, 0x001BF376), is added to a fragment-spread accumulator: the upper measure to the accumulator of the fragment above the boundary (starting index plus offset, 0x001BF363) and the lower measure to that of the fragment below (that index plus fragments across, 0x001BF390). The column is smoothed when both measures are below the activity limit and the two samples adjacent to the boundary differ by less than the quantizer step in both directions. Where it applies, the flat smoothing replaces each of the eight run samples by the sum of the three samples to its left, the three to its right and twice itself, plus 4, arithmetically shifted right 3. Window positions outside the run on the left all take the value of the sample five positions before the edge, and those outside on the right take the sample four positions after the edge (these two outer samples are read in addition to the run). All inputs are original values (rounding at 0x001BF3F4, shifts at 0x001BF3FA..0x001BF4B6), the eight results being written vertically to the destination column. Otherwise only the two boundary samples are written through the short filter (a difference is formed from the four samples nearest the edge as (second-left) minus three times (first-left) plus three times (first-right) minus (second-right), 4 is added and the result arithmetically shifted right 3. That value indexes the deblock bounding array (field 0x38, relative to its centre) and the looked-up amount is added to the first-left sample and subtracted from the first-right sample, each result taken through the clamp table, 0x001BF4DD..0x001BF50F) and the other six destination samples receive copies of the source samples.
Vertical edge on the left of the upper fragment (starting index plus offset, band above the boundary), skipped for offset 0. It is processed in place in the destination buffer one band (8 line steps) above the band pointer, at the pixel column 8 times the offset: the same ten-sample rule per line with horizontal neighbours, the capped activity measures added to the accumulators of the fragment to the left (index minus 1) and of the upper fragment itself (0x001BF69F..0x001BF6EC), flat smoothing written horizontally when the test passes (0x001BF749..0x001BF7D9), otherwise the short filter on the two adjacent samples (0x001BF7F2..0x001BF816).
