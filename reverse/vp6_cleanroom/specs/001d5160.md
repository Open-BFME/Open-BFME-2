## Behaviour

Notation used below: inputs of a 1-D transform are in0..in7, its outputs out0..out7, intermediate terms are named by role, multiplier constants by their retail value (M[c](v) = floor(v x c / 65536), see the multiplier notation paragraph), sat() = clamp to -32768..32767, wrap() = modulo 65536.
Three-coefficient inverse DCT with no dequantisation (nothing in retail references it). The output cache lines are touched first (dword loads at output byte offsets 0, 28, 56, 84, 112, 124, 0x001D516B-0x001D5179). Input words 0, 1, 2 are placed with the lane masks into natural positions (0,0), (0,1) and (1,0) of the working buffer (0x001D5198-0x001D51D1) and the remaining rows are zeroed (sixteen 8-byte stores, 0x001D51D5-0x001D520D). Then natural rows 0 and 1 are transformed with only inputs in0 and in1 present (0x001D5211-0x001D5284), the 4x4 transposes follow, and every natural column is transformed with only rows 0 and 1 as inputs, with rounding and arithmetic shift right 4 (shifts at 0x001D5353-0x001D53A3 and 0x001D540C-0x001D545D). 64 raster outputs are written.

Value contract (all arithmetic WRAPPING modulo 65536 - the body has 46 paddw and 15 psubw and no saturating instruction, multiplier notation as in 0x001D35E0, M[46341] add-backs wrapping):
- two-input 1-D transform of in0 (position 0) and in1 (position 1): termA = M[64277](in1), termB = M[12785](in1), rotA = M[46341](termA), rotB = M[46341](termB), termE = M[46341](in0).
  first pass: out7 = wrap(termE - termA), out0 = wrap(wrap(termA + termA) + out7), termU = wrap(termE - rotA) (the even term minus the rotated first odd term), termV = wrap(wrap(rotA + rotA) + termU) (the even term plus the rotated first odd term), out2 = wrap(termV - rotB), out1 = wrap(wrap(rotB + rotB) + out2), out6 = wrap(termU - rotB), out5 = wrap(wrap(rotB + rotB) + out6), out4 = wrap(termE - termB), out3 = wrap(wrap(termB + termB) + out4). So out1 and out2 carry the rotated first odd term with a plus sign and out5 and out6 with a minus sign (first-pass combination sites 0x001D525A-0x001D5278), which is what makes the horizontal check vector below read 3, 2, 2, 1, -1, -2, -2, -3.
  second pass: identical, except that termE is replaced by wrap(termE + 8) before every difference (so the rounding enters termU, termV and the out7/out4 differences alike), and every output is arithmetically shifted right by 4.
- in the second pass in0 and in1 of a column are the first-pass outputs of natural rows 0 and 1 for that column.
