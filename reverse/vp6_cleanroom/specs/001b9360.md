name: VP6_AllocateFrameInfo
rva: 0x001B9360
size: 167
ret_site: 0x001B9406 (also 0x001B93F2)
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to PB_INSTANCE (retail decoder instance, 0x4954 bytes), [esp+8] frame size in bytes
returns: int, 1 on success, 0 on failure
callees: 0x001B92D0 VP6_DeleteFrameInfo, 0x001B6400 duck_malloc
tables: none
fields: 0x1B8 Y stride, and the raw/aligned pairs 0x248/0x244, 0x250/0x24C, 0x258/0x254

Behaviour:
Calls VP6_DeleteFrameInfo, then allocates three reconstruction frames of (frame size plus Y stride plus 32) bytes each: this-frame (0x248/0x244), golden (0x250/0x24C), last (0x258/0x254). The post-process buffer is not allocated here (it is created lazily by VP6_GetYUVConfig). A null result causes VP6_DeleteFrameInfo and 0. Otherwise 1.
