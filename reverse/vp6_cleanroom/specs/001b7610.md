name: VP6_quantize_c
rva: 0x001B7610
size: 500
ret_site: 0x001B7803
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to QUANTIZER (retail quantizer instance, 0x990 bytes), [esp+8] pointer to 64 signed-16 DCT coefficients (raster order), [esp+0xC] pointer to 64 signed-16 output, [esp+0x10] block position byte (0..5)
returns: void
callees: none
tables: 0x00BD8760 plane selector (6 bytes 0,0,0,0,1,1, read at 0x001B7615), 0x00BD8460 zig-zag order (64 dwords: scan position to raster position, read at 0x001B7690 and following)
fields: 0x190 multipliers, 0x390 rounding values, 0x590 zero-bin sizes, 0x790 zero-run corrections (each 2 planes of 64 dwords, plane row stride 0x100)

Behaviour:
Forward-quantizes one block into scan order with dead-zone rounding. The plane row of the constant tables is chosen by the block position through the selector. All 64 outputs start at zero.
DC (raster and scan position 0): a coefficient at or above the plane's DC zero-bin size is increased by the DC rounding value, multiplied by the DC multiplier and the high 16 bits of the product kept (0x001B7653..0x001B765A). A coefficient at or below the negative of the zero-bin size is decreased by the rounding value, multiplied, 0xFFFF added and the high 16 bits kept (rounding toward zero for negatives, 0x001B7675). Otherwise the output stays zero and the run of zeros starts at 1.
AC: scan positions 1..63 are visited in order, each mapping to its raster position through the zig-zag table. A position's effective dead zone is the plane's zero-bin size for that raster position plus the zero-run correction for the current length of the run of zeros (0x001B7699..0x001B76AE). A coefficient reaching the dead zone (or at or below its negation) is quantized with the same rounding, multiplier and high-16-bit rule as the DC, stored at the scan position, and the zero run resets to 0, otherwise the output stays zero and the run grows by one (kept in a byte). Retail processes three scan positions per loop iteration.
