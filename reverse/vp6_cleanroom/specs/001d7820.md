## Behaviour

SSE2 version of the four-tap one-dimensional filter with the same contract as 0x001D6C90: for each of 'output height' rows, eight outputs are formed from four weighted samples (weights from the four eight-entry groups at byte offsets 0, 16, 32 and 48 of the taps pointer, applying to the samples at x-1, x, x+1 and x+2 of the row), summed in eight 16-bit lanes, plus the rounding constant 64 from the listed table, arithmetically shifted right 7 (0x001D78A3) and packed to bytes with unsigned saturation. Source rows advance by 'source pixels per line', output rows by 'output width' bytes, 'pixel step' is unused. Loads are unaligned 128-bit.
