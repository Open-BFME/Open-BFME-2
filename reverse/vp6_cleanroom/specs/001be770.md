Behaviour:
Band deblocker for the non-loop-filtered profile. For each fragment of the band in order:
Horizontal edge at the top of the band. For the horizontal edge the band pointer addresses the first row below the boundary, so the fragment whose rows start at the band pointer is physically below the boundary and its index is the starting index plus fragments across, while the starting index itself names the fragment above the boundary (the previous band). The quantizer step is the scale entry for the Q index of the lower fragment (the starting index plus the fragment's offset plus fragments across). Each of the 8 columns is a line of eight vertical samples around the band boundary. Edge test per line: for each side of the edge, the sum of the four samples and the sum of their squares are formed and the side's spread measure is the sum of squares minus the product of half the sum (rounded down) and half the sum plus one (rounded down). Both spread measures are added into the fragment-spread accumulators (field 0x28) of the two fragments meeting at the edge. The line is smoothed only when both spread measures are below the spread limit and the two samples adjacent to the edge differ by less than the quantizer step in both directions. The quantizer step is the scale-table entry for the fragment's Q index and the spread limit is the step squared, times 3, arithmetically shifted right 5 (limit at 0x001BED4F..0x001BED52), the measure of the four samples above the boundary being added to the accumulator of the upper fragment (starting index plus offset) and the measure of the four below to the accumulator of the lower fragment (that index plus fragments across). The smoothing replaces each of the eight samples of the run (four on each side of the edge) by a weighted sum over nine consecutive positions centred on it, divided by 16: the centre sample has weight 4, the nearest sample on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 1 and the outermost on each side weight 1. 8 is added before the division (retail adds 4 to a partial sum which is then doubled, so the effective rounding is 8) and the division is an arithmetic shift right by 4. Positions of the nine-sample window that fall outside the run on the left are all taken as the left guard, and those outside on the right as the right guard. The left guard is the sample immediately before the run when it differs from the first run sample by less than the quantizer step in magnitude, otherwise the first run sample. The right guard is the sample immediately after the run when it differs from the last run sample by less than the quantizer step, otherwise the last run sample. All nine inputs are the original values, so the eight outputs may be computed in any order (final shifts right 4 at 0x001BEAB1 and 0x001BEBBF), written vertically. When the line is not smoothed, only the two samples adjacent to the edge change: a difference is formed from the four samples nearest the edge as (second-left) minus three times (first-left) plus three times (first-right) minus (second-right), 4 is added and the result arithmetically shifted right 3. That value indexes the deblock bounding array (field 0x38, relative to its centre) and the looked-up amount is added to the first-left sample and subtracted from the first-right sample, each result taken through the clamp table (0x001BEBE3..0x001BEC15), and the other six destination samples of the column receive copies of the source samples.
Vertical edge on the left of the upper fragment (the one named by the starting index plus offset, in the band above the boundary), skipped for the first fragment: processed in place in the destination one band above the band pointer, at the pixel column 8 times the offset, the quantizer step from that upper fragment's Q index, the same per-line test accumulating into the fragment to its left (index minus 1) and into it, nine-sample smoothing horizontally when the line passes (final shift at 0x001BEFA1), otherwise the short filter on the two samples adjacent to the edge (0x001BEECF..0x001BEFE1).
