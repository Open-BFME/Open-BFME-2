name: VP6_AllocateFragmentInfo
rva: 0x001B9140
size: 396
ret_site: 0x001B92CB (also 0x001B92B8)
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to PB_INSTANCE (retail decoder instance, 0x4954 bytes)
returns: int, 1 on success, 0 on failure
callees: 0x001B9040 VP6_DeleteFragmentInfo, 0x001B6400 duck_malloc
tables: none
fields: 0x1F8 horizontal fragments, 0x228 macroblock count, 0x1FC unit fragments, plus the raw/aligned pairs of the delete spec

Behaviour:
Calls VP6_DeleteFragmentInfo, then allocates (tag 0, raw pointer then 32-byte-rounded pointer):
 1. 0x320 bytes coefficient buffer: raw 0x0, aligned 0x4.
 2. Above-Y contexts: (horizontal fragments plus 10) times 16 bytes: raw 0x118, aligned 0x10C.
 3. Above-U contexts: (horizontal fragments halved plus 10) times 16: raw 0x11C, aligned 0x110.
 4. Above-V contexts: same size: raw 0x120, aligned 0x114.
 5. Interlaced flags: macroblocks plus 32 bytes: raw 0x6F8, aligned 0x6EC.
 6. Prediction modes: macroblocks plus 32 bytes: raw 0x6FC, aligned 0x6F0.
 7. Motion vectors: 4 times macroblocks plus 32: raw 0x700, aligned 0x6F4.
 8. Fragment info: 4 times unit fragments plus 32: raw 0x14C, aligned 0x148.
Any null result triggers VP6_DeleteFragmentInfo and a 0 return. Otherwise 1.
Memory goes through the duck-memory shim: allocate = 0x001B6400 (size, tag), free = 0x001B6410. Every aligned buffer is kept twice: the raw allocation pointer and the pointer rounded up to the next 32-byte boundary (add 31, clear low five bits).
