Behaviour:
Thresholds (immediate stores at 0x001C3459..0x001C3493): version 5 and up uses T1 384, T2 2304, T3 2880, T4 5760, otherwise T1 2048, T2 30720, T3 92160, T4 122880. The quality passed to every block filter call is the frame Q index for all three planes (this routine never consults the per-fragment Q index array, chroma calls at 0x001C39EB and 0x001C3B2B reuse the saved frame value). The block index runs over all fragments in Y, U, V order.
Luma is processed as two fields with line length twice the Y stride and half the vertical fragments per field: first the even field at the recon Y offset, then the odd field one Y stride further, the block index continuing across both. Per luma block with spread measure V: if the level exceeds 5 and V exceeds T3, the strong filter is applied once, and twice more if any existing left, right, below or above neighbour block (within the field) has a measure above T4. Else if V exceeds T2, strong once. Else if V exceeds T1, weak once. Else the block is copied.
Chroma planes use line length equal to a quarter of the doubled Y stride, half the horizontal fragments across, and the field row count as the row count. Per chroma block: if the level exceeds 5 and V exceeds T4, strong three times, else T2 gives strong once, T1 gives weak once, else copy.
