Behaviour:
Two-dimensional resampling of a plane through a temporary area, the horizontal pass feeding the vertical pass.
Line routines: horizontally, the point-sampling 2-to-1 routine is used when the horizontal scale is 2 and the horizontal ratio is 1 (0x001BA51A..0x001BA539), otherwise the generic linear routine. Vertically, when the vertical scale is 2 and the vertical ratio is 1, the point-sampling 2-to-1 routine is used for interlaced input and the three-tap 2-to-1 routine otherwise (0x001BA544..0x001BA562), any other vertical pair uses the generic routine. Every horizontal call receives (source row, step 1, horizontal scale, source width plus 1 as the length, output row, step 1, horizontal ratio, destination width). Every vertical call receives (temporary column, destination pitch as the step, vertical scale, source band height plus 1, destination column, destination pitch, vertical ratio, destination band height).
Same height: when the source and destination heights are equal, each destination row is simply the horizontal resampling of the corresponding source row, written directly into the destination (loop at 0x001BA590). No temporary rows are used.
Different height: the plane is processed in bands. When the destination is taller than the source, the destination band height is the temporary height minus 1 and the source band height is that times the source height divided by the destination height (unsigned, 0x001BA5D6). Otherwise the source band height is the temporary height minus 1 and the destination band height is that times the vertical ratio divided by the vertical scale (0x001BA5F6). The number of bands is the destination height plus the destination band height minus 1, divided by the destination band height (0x001BA62B), and nothing further happens when that is 0. The temporary area holds source-band-height plus 1 horizontally resampled rows at the destination pitch: row 0 is the resampling of the first source row of the band, rows 1 to source-band-height are the resamplings of the following source rows, and any source row index at or beyond the source height is replaced by a duplicate of the previous temporary row (compare at 0x001BA6BA, copy at 0x001BA6EC of destination-pitch bytes). Each destination column of the band is then produced from the corresponding temporary column by the vertical routine. Between bands, the last temporary row (row source-band-height) becomes the next band's row 0 (copy at 0x001BA787, destination-pitch bytes), the source position advances by source-band-height rows and the destination by destination-band-height rows. Row 0 of the very first band is resampled before the band loop (call at 0x001BA621). Note that the temporary area is written as destination-pitch-wide rows, so it must hold (temporary height) times (destination pitch) bytes.
