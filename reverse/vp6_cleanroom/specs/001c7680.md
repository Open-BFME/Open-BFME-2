## Behaviour

MMX band deblocker for bands whose source was already loop-filtered. Two passes over one band of blocks.

Pass 1, horizontal block edge (between the block above and the block whose first row the pointers address), for each block k of the band, eight columns at a time: the quantiser step q is the scale-table entry for the quality index of the block directly below the edge (the per-block quality array at +0x24 is read one band-width beyond the band's start index, at the block's position). If q is 3 or less (compare at 0x001C76E2) the eight source rows -4..+3 of the block's eight columns are copied to the destination unchanged. Otherwise the edge model below is evaluated per column from source rows -5..+4, the upper side's activity is added to the accumulator (+0x28) of the block above the edge (at the band's start index plus the block's position) and the lower side's activity to the accumulator of the block below it (one band-width further on), smoothed columns receive their eight new values, other columns are copied unchanged. Pointers advance by 8 per block.

Pass 2, vertical block edges, in place on the destination eight rows above the pointer row (the rows pass 1 just wrote): for each adjacent pair (k, k+1), q is the scale-table entry for the quality index of the right-hand block of the pair. If q is 3 or less nothing is done. Otherwise, per row, the ten pixels at columns -5..+4 of the edge are read from the destination into a staging array of 16-bit values, the edge model is applied, the left side's activity is added to the left block's accumulator and the right side's to the right block's, and smoothed rows are written back, rows that are not smoothed are left as they are.

Edge model shared by the variance-based band deblockers (one line crossing a block edge, eight edge pixels: four on each side, plus one further pixel outward on each side, called the outer samples, q is the quantiser step of the block below/right of the edge):
- smoothing limit = (3 x q x q) shifted right 5 (retail: two 16-bit multiplies by q with the 16-bit 3s constant then a logical shift right 5, e.g. 0x001C770C-0x001C7712 and 0x001C8AF1-0x001C8B14).
- outer taps: on each side the outer sample is used if its absolute difference from the adjacent edge pixel is below q, otherwise that adjacent edge pixel is used instead.
- side activity: for each side, the sum of its four pixels and the sum of their squares are formed and the activity is the sum of squares minus the product of (sum halved, rounded down) and (sum plus one, halved, rounded down). The activity of the first side is added into the first block's accumulator at +0x28, that of the second side into the second block's.
- a line is 'smoothed' when both activities are below the smoothing limit and the absolute difference of the two pixels touching the edge is below q.
- smoothing: each of the eight edge pixels is replaced by a symmetric nine-tap weighted average centred on it with weights 1, 1, 2, 2, 4, 2, 2, 1, 1 (total 16, rounding constant 8 is applied as the 16-bit 4s constant added before a doubling, final arithmetic shift right 4 at e.g. 0x001C7AC5 / 0x001CF832), window positions that fall beyond the first or last edge pixel take the value of the corresponding outer tap. Results are stored as bytes (unsigned pack).

Processing is eight columns (pass 1) or eight rows (pass 2) per step, the per-side activity is accumulated in 16-bit lanes and summed from two four-lane partials. No MMX state clear is executed by this routine, and both pass-1 paths use MMX. The copy shortcut taken when q is 3 or less (branch at 0x001C76E5 to 0x001C7F28) copies the block's eight rows with eight 8-byte MMX loads and eight MMX stores (0x001C7F3C to 0x001C7F84), and the filtering path uses MMX from 0x001C7701. A band of one or more blocks therefore returns with the MMX register state in use whatever the quality steps are, including a one-block band at quality step 3 (the separately installed clear-state routine at 0x001D7620 is the caller's cleanup). A block count of zero is outside this contract: the loop counts are unsigned, and a bounded retail run with zero blocks did not reach the return.
