Behaviour:
For each of 8 lines, with four pixels two on each side of the edge: a correction starts as three times the step across the edge (right-adjacent minus left-adjacent). The line counts as high-activity when either pair of same-side pixels (the two left ones, or the two right ones) differs by more than 1 in magnitude (0x001C3F7F, 0x001C3F94). For a high-activity line the correction also gains the outermost left pixel minus the outermost right pixel. The correction has 4 added, is arithmetically shifted right 3 (0x001C3FAE..0x001C3FB1), and indexes the bounding array from its centre (0x001C3FB4), the looked-up amount is added to the left-adjacent pixel and subtracted from the right-adjacent one through the clamp table. For a line that is not high-activity, half of that amount (arithmetic halving, 0x001C3FD3) is additionally added to the outermost left pixel and subtracted from the outermost right pixel. The pointer advances by the line length per line.
