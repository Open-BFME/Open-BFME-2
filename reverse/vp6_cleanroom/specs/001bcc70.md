name: VP6_StopEncode
rva: 0x001BCC70

ret_site: 0x001BCCE1
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to bool coder state (0x0 low value, 0x4 range, 0x8 value, 0xC count, 0x10 byte position, 0x14 buffer pointer, 0x18 measure-cost flag, 0x1C bit counter)
returns: void
callees: none
tables: none
fields: none

Behaviour:
Flushes the encoder. Shift amount = 24 minus (count and 7) when count is below minus 16, 16 minus (count and 7) when below minus 8, else 8 minus (count and 7). The low value is shifted left by that amount, then its four bytes are written to the buffer at the position, most significant first, advancing the position by four, and the position is advanced once more without writing a byte.
