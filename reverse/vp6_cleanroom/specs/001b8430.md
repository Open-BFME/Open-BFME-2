Behaviour:
Separable bilinear 8x8 filter. The horizontal pass is inlined: for 9 consecutive source rows (advancing by the pixels per line), each of 8 output positions is the first weight times the pixel at that position plus the second weight times its right neighbour, plus 64, arithmetically shifted right 7, stored as a dword in the scratch buffer row. Then the vertical pass routine is called on the scratch buffer with (scratch, output, 8 entries per line, step 8, 8 rows, 8 columns, vertical weights).
