name: VP6_VPInitLibrary
rva: 0x001B57D0
size: 90
ret_site: tail jump at 0x001B5825 to 0x001B8040 (no ret of its own, then CC padding)
convention: cdecl, no arguments
args: none
returns: void
callees: 0x001B8E90 VP6_GetProcessorFrequency, 0x001B8FB0 VP6_DMachineSpecificConfig, 0x001B6520 InitPostProcessing, 0x001B8040 InitVPUtil (tail call)
tables: 0x00BD7E60 DC quantizer table, 64 signed 16-bit entries (values listed in the VP6_init_quantizer spec)
        0x00DFD950 writable 64-dword DC quant scale table (bss), 0x00E23300 global CPU frequency (dword)
fields: none

Behaviour:
Library-wide one-time setup. Stores the processor frequency returned by 0x001B8E90 into the global at 0x00E23300. Calls VP6_DMachineSpecificConfig. Then for each of the 64 quantizer indices computes the DC quantizer entry (signed 16-bit at 0x00BD7E60) divided by two with truncation toward zero, plus two, and stores it as a dword into the table at 0x00DFD950.
Then calls InitPostProcessing with that same table passed three times (as the V2, UV and V1 scale tables) and the version number 7. Finally tail-jumps to InitVPUtil.
