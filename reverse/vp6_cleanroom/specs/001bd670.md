Behaviour:
Smooths the vertical edges between horizontally adjacent fragments of one band: (fragments across minus 1) edges, each 8 pixels further into the band, the quantizer step being the scale-table entry for the Q index of the fragment on the right of the edge (0x001BD6B5..0x001BD6C0). For each of the 8 lines the eight samples straddling the edge are examined. Edge test per line: for each side of the edge, the sum of the four samples and the sum of their squares are formed and the side's spread measure is the sum of squares minus the product of half the sum (rounded down) and half the sum plus one (rounded down). Both spread measures are added into the fragment-spread accumulators (field 0x28) of the two fragments meeting at the edge. The line is smoothed only when both spread measures are below the spread limit and the two samples adjacent to the edge differ by less than the quantizer step in both directions. The quantizer step is the scale-table entry for the fragment's Q index and the spread limit is the step squared, times 3, arithmetically shifted right 5 (spread limit at 0x001BD6CA..0x001BD6CD, accumulator updates at 0x001BD794 and 0x001BD79D). The smoothing replaces each of the eight samples of the run (four on each side of the edge) by a weighted sum over nine consecutive positions centred on it, divided by 16: the centre sample has weight 4, the nearest sample on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 1 and the outermost on each side weight 1. 8 is added before the division (retail adds 4 to a partial sum which is then doubled, so the effective rounding is 8) and the division is an arithmetic shift right by 4. Positions of the nine-sample window that fall outside the run on the left are all taken as the left guard, and those outside on the right as the right guard. The left guard is the sample immediately before the run when it differs from the first run sample by less than the quantizer step in magnitude, otherwise the first run sample. The right guard is the sample immediately after the run when it differs from the last run sample by less than the quantizer step, otherwise the last run sample. All nine inputs are the original values, so the eight outputs may be computed in any order (partial rounding 4 at 0x001BD830, doubling at 0x001BD836, final shifts right 4 at 0x001BD83C, 0x001BD85D, 0x001BD8E1 and 0x001BD91C). Lines that fail the test are left unchanged in the destination.
