Behaviour:
Copies the 14-dword configuration into field 0x40. Derives: horizontal fragments = width shifted right 3, vertical fragments = height shifted right 3, Y stride and UV stride from the configuration, Y plane fragments = horizontal times vertical, UV plane fragments = Y plane fragments shifted right 2, unit fragments = Y plane fragments plus twice the UV plane fragments, border = (Y stride minus 8 times horizontal fragments) shifted right 1.
Recon Y offset = border times Y stride plus border. Recon U offset = Y stride times (height plus twice the border) plus half the border times UV stride plus half the border. Recon V offset = that same Y-plane size plus UV stride times (half the height plus the border) plus half the border times UV stride plus half the border. All divisions are unsigned shifts.
