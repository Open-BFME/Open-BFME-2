# VerticalBand_3_5_Scale_MMX

name: VerticalBand_3_5_Scale_MMX
rva: 0x001D23E0
size: 366
ret_site: 0x001D254D
convention: cdecl, 3 stack args, caller cleans (ebp frame)
args: band pointer (bytes, row 0 of a 6-row band) | pitch (uint) | width (uint)
returns: none
callees: none
tables: 0x00BD9910 four 16-bit 51 | 0x00BD9920 102 | 0x00BD9930 154 | 0x00BD9940 205 | 0x00BD9950 128
fields: none

## Behaviour

Vertical 3-to-5 interpolation in place over a band of six rows (rows 0 and 5 are the bounding source rows). Column by column on the original values: row 1 becomes the blend of rows 0 and 1 with weights 102 and 154, row 2 the blend of rows 1 and 2 with weights 205 and 51, row 3 the blend of the same rows 1 and 2 with weights 51 and 205, row 4 the blend of rows 2 and 5 with weights 154 and 102. Each blend is the weighted sum plus 128, shifted right 8, in 16-bit lanes, packed to bytes. Rows 0 and 5 are unchanged. Four columns per step, width rounded up to a multiple of 4. No MMX state clear is executed by this routine: it leaves the MMX register state in use on return (the separately installed clear-state routine at 0x001D7620 is the caller's cleanup).
