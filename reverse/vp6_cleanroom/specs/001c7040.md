fields: 0x72C transmitted probabilities, 3 types of 2 rows of 10 bytes (row 0 at 0x72C plus 20 times type, row 1 at 0x736 plus 20 times type), 0x77C same-mode probabilities (10 per type), 0x7A4 mode-tree probabilities (9 per (type, mode) pair). Retail constants: weight 100 at 0x001C7099, scale 255 at 0x001C70B5 and following, signed division at 0x001C70BC, unsigned divisions at 0x001C70EE onward, all results stored as low bytes.

Behaviour:
Derives the mode-decoding tree probabilities from the transmitted statistics. For each previous mode (0..9) and each context type (0..2):
A weighted count is formed for every mode: 100 times the type's row-0 entry for that mode, except that the previous mode itself counts 0. The grand total of the ten counts is also kept.
The same-mode probability of the type and previous mode is 255 minus the quotient of 255 times the type's row-1 entry for the previous mode by (1 plus that row-1 entry plus the row-0 entry for the previous mode).
The nine tree entries are each 1 plus the quotient of 255 times a group's count by (1 plus a larger group's count), expressed with the mode groups:
 entry 0: modes 0, 2, 3, 4 together over all ten modes.
 entry 1: modes 0 and 2 over modes 0, 2, 3, 4.
 entry 2: modes 1 and 7 over modes 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.
 entry 3: mode 0 over modes 0 and 2.
 entry 4: mode 3 over modes 3 and 4.
 entry 5: mode 1 over modes 1 and 7.
 entry 6: modes 5 and 6 over modes 5, 6, 8, 9.
 entry 7: mode 5 over modes 5 and 6.
 entry 8: mode 8 over modes 8 and 9.
