## Behaviour

SSE2 version of the four-tap one-dimensional filter with the same contract as 0x001D6D70: for each of 'output height' rows, eight outputs are formed from four weighted samples (weights from the four eight-entry groups at byte offsets 0, 16, 32 and 48 of the taps pointer, applying to the same column in the rows one above, at, one below and two below), summed in eight 16-bit lanes, plus the rounding constant 64 from the listed table, arithmetically shifted right 7 (the shift-right-7 site of the body) and packed to bytes with unsigned saturation. Source rows advance by 'source pixels per line', output rows by 'output width' bytes, 'pixel step' is unused. Loads are unaligned 128-bit.
