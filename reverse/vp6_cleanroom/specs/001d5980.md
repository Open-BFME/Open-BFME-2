## Behaviour

Half-pixel inter reconstruction without SIMD: scratch = ((reference1 + reference2) shifted right 1, computed in 32-bit) + residual for each of 64 positions (both references advance by the line step per row, residual/scratch by 8), then SatUnsigned8(output, scratch, line step, 8).
