Behaviour:
Band deblocker for the loop-filtered profile, two passes.
Pass 1, the horizontal edge at the top of the band: For the horizontal edge the band pointer addresses the first row below the boundary, so the fragment whose rows start at the band pointer is physically below the boundary and its index is the starting index plus fragments across (its Q index is read at 0x001BDA70..0x001BDA81 for the first fragment), while the starting index itself names the fragment above the boundary (the previous band). For each fragment in order, the quantizer step is the scale entry for the Q index of the lower fragment (starting index plus the fragment's offset plus fragments across), and each of the 8 columns is treated as a line of eight vertical samples, four rows above the band boundary and four from it downward. Edge test per line: for each side of the edge, the sum of the four samples and the sum of their squares are formed and the side's spread measure is the sum of squares minus the product of half the sum (rounded down) and half the sum plus one (rounded down). Both spread measures are added into the fragment-spread accumulators (field 0x28) of the two fragments meeting at the edge. The line is smoothed only when both spread measures are below the spread limit and the two samples adjacent to the edge differ by less than the quantizer step in both directions. The quantizer step is the scale-table entry for the fragment's Q index and the spread limit is the step squared, times 3, arithmetically shifted right 5 (limit at 0x001BDA8B..0x001BDA8E), the spread measure of the four samples above the boundary being added to the accumulator of the upper fragment (the starting index plus the fragment's offset) and the measure of the four samples below to the accumulator of the lower fragment (that index plus fragments across). The smoothing replaces each of the eight samples of the run (four on each side of the edge) by a weighted sum over nine consecutive positions centred on it, divided by 16: the centre sample has weight 4, the nearest sample on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 1 and the outermost on each side weight 1. 8 is added before the division (retail adds 4 to a partial sum which is then doubled, so the effective rounding is 8) and the division is an arithmetic shift right by 4. Positions of the nine-sample window that fall outside the run on the left are all taken as the left guard, and those outside on the right as the right guard. The left guard is the sample immediately before the run when it differs from the first run sample by less than the quantizer step in magnitude, otherwise the first run sample. The right guard is the sample immediately after the run when it differs from the last run sample by less than the quantizer step, otherwise the last run sample. All nine inputs are the original values, so the eight outputs may be computed in any order (final shifts right 4 at 0x001BDC64, 0x001BDD1F and 0x001BDD6B), written vertically into the destination, a column that fails the test has its eight source samples copied unchanged into the destination.
Pass 2, the vertical edges of the band above the boundary (the fragments named by the starting index onward): for each pair of horizontally adjacent fragments, operating in place on the destination one band (8 line steps) above the band pointer, the quantizer step from the Q index of the right fragment of the pair, the spread limit recomputed (0x001BDE9B), and the same per-line test and nine-sample smoothing applied horizontally (final shift at 0x001BE0DF), accumulating into the two fragments of the pair. Failing lines are left unchanged.
