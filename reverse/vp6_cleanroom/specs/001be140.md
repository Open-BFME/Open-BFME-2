tables: clamp table: 768 bytes allocated at 0x00E23000..0x00E232FF with its zero point at 0x00E23100, so the byte at the zero point plus a signed offset s (s from -256 to 511) holds s clamped to 0..255. fields: 0x24 fragment Q index array, 0x28 fragment-spread accumulators, 0x38 deblock value pointer (centre of the bounding array)

Behaviour:
Vertical edges between horizontally adjacent fragments of one band ((fragments across minus 1) edges), the quantizer step from the Q index of the right fragment, each of the 8 lines examined over the eight samples straddling the edge. Edge test per line: for each side of the edge, the sum of the four samples and the sum of their squares are formed and the side's spread measure is the sum of squares minus the product of half the sum (rounded down) and half the sum plus one (rounded down). Both spread measures are added into the fragment-spread accumulators (field 0x28) of the two fragments meeting at the edge. The line is smoothed only when both spread measures are below the spread limit and the two samples adjacent to the edge differ by less than the quantizer step in both directions. The quantizer step is the scale-table entry for the fragment's Q index and the spread limit is the step squared, times 3, arithmetically shifted right 5 (limit at 0x001BE19D..0x001BE1A0). The smoothing replaces each of the eight samples of the run (four on each side of the edge) by a weighted sum over nine consecutive positions centred on it, divided by 16: the centre sample has weight 4, the nearest sample on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 2, the next on each side weight 1 and the outermost on each side weight 1. 8 is added before the division (retail adds 4 to a partial sum which is then doubled, so the effective rounding is 8) and the division is an arithmetic shift right by 4. Positions of the nine-sample window that fall outside the run on the left are all taken as the left guard, and those outside on the right as the right guard. The left guard is the sample immediately before the run when it differs from the first run sample by less than the quantizer step in magnitude, otherwise the first run sample. The right guard is the sample immediately after the run when it differs from the last run sample by less than the quantizer step, otherwise the last run sample. All nine inputs are the original values, so the eight outputs may be computed in any order (final shifts right 4 at 0x001BE322, 0x001BE3A6, 0x001BE3F4). When the line is not smoothed, only the two samples adjacent to the edge change: a difference is formed from the four samples nearest the edge as (second-left) minus three times (first-left) plus three times (first-right) minus (second-right), 4 is added and the result arithmetically shifted right 3. That value indexes the deblock bounding array (field 0x38, relative to its centre) and the looked-up amount is added to the first-left sample and subtracted from the first-right sample, each result taken through the clamp table (0x001BE3E5..0x001BE414, stores at 0x001BE421 and 0x001BE435).
