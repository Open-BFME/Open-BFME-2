name: VP6_PickPostProcessingLevel
rva: 0x001B5AC0
size: 404
ret_site: 0x001B5C53 (multiple earlier rets)
convention: cdecl
args: [esp+4] pointer to PB_INSTANCE (retail decoder instance)
returns: int post-processing level (0, 4, 5, 6, 8, or the current level)
callees: none
tables: none. Immediates: 31000 at 0x001B5B55, 30000 at 0x001B5BCD, 28000 at 0x001B5BE3 and 0x001B5C00, divisions by 100 done with the multiplier 0x51EB851F and a shift of 5 (0x001B5B5B, 0x001B5BD3, 0x001B5BE9, 0x001B5C06)
fields: 0x1A0 current post-processing level, 0x1A8 CPU-free percentage, 0x910 this-frame decode time, 0x914 average decode time, 0x918 array of 10 average post-process times indexed by level, 0x940 average blit time

Behaviour:
Adaptive level selection from timing statistics. Three costs are formed: the baseline cost is this frame's decode time plus the average blit time plus the stored cost of level 8. The projected cost is the baseline cost plus the stored cost of the current level. The average cost is the average decode time plus the average blit time.
Missing statistics are seeded: when the stored cost of level 6 or of level 5 is zero it becomes half the average cost, when the level 4 cost is zero it becomes a quarter of the average cost, when the level 8 cost is zero it becomes an eighth of the average cost (arithmetic shifts).
If the CPU-free percentage is 0 the current level is returned unchanged.
Three watermarks scale with the CPU-free percentage: the critical watermark is 31000 times it divided by 100, the lower watermark 30000 times it divided by 100, the upper watermark 28000 times it divided by 100 (unsigned divisions).
If the projected cost exceeds the critical watermark: the average decode time is replaced by this frame's decode time, then the first level among 6, 5, 4, 8 whose stored cost plus the baseline cost is below the critical watermark is returned, else 0.
Otherwise, if the projected cost lies strictly between the upper and lower watermarks, the current level is returned.
Otherwise the first level among 6, 5, 4, 8 whose stored cost plus the average cost is below the upper watermark is returned, else 0.
